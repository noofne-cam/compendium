Given $$\text ds^2 = g_{\mu\nu}dx^\mu dx^\nu,$$the Christoffel Symbols are the components of the canonical connection: $$\Gamma^\lambda_{\mu\nu} = \frac{1}{2}g^{\lambda\rho}(g_{\mu\lambda,\nu} + g_{\lambda\nu,\mu} - g_{\mu\nu,\lambda}).$$

---
### **I.** For the **Euclidean Plane** $\mathbf R^2$, Polar Coordinates ($n$ = 6; $r,\phi \to 1,2$)
$$\begin{matrix} \Gamma^1_{11} = 0 & \Gamma^2_{11} = 0 \\ \Gamma^1_{12} = 0 & \Gamma^2_{12} = r^{-1} \\ \Gamma^1_{22} = -r & \Gamma^2_{22} = 0  \end{matrix}$$
